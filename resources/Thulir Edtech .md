# **Thulir Edtech**

This is a mobile application and web platform where children get the right education at the right time in the right way.

**Thulir Edtech** is an innovative educational platform designed for children under the age of 12 to learn **Mathematics**, **Environmental Science**, and selected **Languages** through creative, interactive, and personalized methods. Rooted in a custom-designed curriculum, Thulir Edtech blends education with engagement using gamified modules and visual storytelling.

At the heart of the platform is **Elvee**, an AI-powered virtual mentor who acts as a **teacher, friend, and guide**, making learning feel like a conversation rather than a classroom. Elvee adapts to each child’s pace and style of learning, encouraging curiosity and building strong foundational understanding in core subjects.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between conventional teaching and modern cognitive development in early education. It is envisioned as a scalable, accessible, and impactful **ed-tech startup**, to be launched and developed during my college years.

## Features:

### **TINY – Teaching Intelligent Nodes to Youngsters**

**TINY** is a custom-designed programming language built using Python, aimed at introducing children under 12 to the core logic and structure of real-world programming. Unlike block-based platforms like Scratch that mainly focus on animation, TINY emphasizes **logic building, problem-solving, and coding mindset**.

Its syntax is designed to mimic **natural English-like sentences**, making it intuitive and digestible for young minds. TINY serves as a stepping stone between playful coding and traditional programming, helping kids gradually transition toward languages like Python, but in a way that doesn't overwhelm them.

TINY is a core part of the Thulir Edtech platform, blending seamlessly with its educational mission.

### Courseware
